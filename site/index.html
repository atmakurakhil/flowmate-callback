AI disclosed in the first sentence

The greeting is built in code, not by the owner, and says "AI" in every language. Each transcript is re-checked after the call. agent.py, analysis.py

No unwritten prices

The agent answers only from the owner's written profile. Any price-like number it says that isn't written down is flagged to the owner as CHECK (lead 5). analysis.audit_call()
